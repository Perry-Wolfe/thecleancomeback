Our story

Drinks got complicated.

Somewhere along the way, functional drinks turned into chemistry projects or wellness products with no personality. PURE is bringing the fun back, with a simpler standard for what goes inside.

L.A. makes the brand. Mexico grows it.

Los Angeles is our studio. It's where we shoot, where we test, and where the first people pick up a can at the gym.

Mexico is our first commercial market. We're building there through real relationships with retailers and distributors, then taking what works across Latin America.

Three identities.
One company.

Each line keeps its own look. We'd rather have three strong personalities than one flattened package system.

  • Pure Energy

    Retro California with modern function. Energy for the real ones.

  • Perry's Protein

    Classic dairy character with serious protein. Nourishment that delivers.

  • Pure Pop

    Bright, caffeine-free and good for you. Pop with benefits.

New flavors, restocks and launch dates, a couple of times a month.
